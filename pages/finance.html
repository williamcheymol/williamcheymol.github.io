Delta & Gamma
Hedging Simulation

European option hedging under Black-Scholes via Monte Carlo (1M paths). Delta and delta-gamma hedging with P&L attribution, transaction costs, and vol mismatch analysis. Extended with a live SPY implied volatility surface (RBF interpolation) to quantify how the vol smile degrades a flat-vol hedge — 4.18% error vs 10.40% on the real surface. 53 unit tests.

SOFR Swap Curve
Calibration & Cap Pricing

Bootstraps a SOFR zero-coupon curve from 9 market swap rates (linear interpolation, cubic spline reverted after documented artifacts). Prices a vanilla interest rate cap via Black-76, with DV01 via full re-bootstrap and Vega by bump-and-reprice. No QuantLib.

Python Bootstrapping Black-76 Fixed Income DV01

Worst-Of Basket
Note Pricing

Monte Carlo pricing of a worst-of basket note on three correlated equity indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Euro Stoxx). Cholesky correlation structure, closed-form GBM terminal simulation, 95% CI, per-asset Delta via common random numbers, and a price-vs-correlation sensitivity study. No QuantLib.
