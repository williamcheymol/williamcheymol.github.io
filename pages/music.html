Live music built from nothing — one layer at a time, in real time, with no safety net.

WHAT IS A LOOPSTATION

A loopstation is a device that records short musical phrases — called loops — and plays them back in real time, continuously. Originally designed as a pedal-based tool for guitarists, modern loopstations have been optimised for hand control, enabling far more precise and expressive manipulation.

The beatbox community was quick to seize on this. With the ability to layer vocals, beatbox rhythms, harmonics and sound design on the fly, beatboxers turned the loopstation into a live composition instrument — building entire tracks from nothing, in front of an audience, with no backing track.

The result is a full arrangement performed by one person, assembled in real time with no ability to undo.

Around this art form, a competitive scene has emerged worldwide. Battles follow a structured format: each artist performs two prepared rounds, judged head-to-head against an opponent. The stakes are real — events like the Grand Beatbox Battle draw thousands of viewers and feature some of the most technically advanced live performances in contemporary music.
